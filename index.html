Waypoints

    No waypoints yet. Add one by hand, or stand at the spot and tap “Use my location”.

    The pram steers itself toward each waypoint in turn and counts a waypoint reached within about 5 m — tighter than that is below what the GPS can resolve. Saying “stop” pauses the route and “go forward” resumes it; other voice commands are ignored while a route runs. Obstacles pause it too. The stop button, Stop route and losing this connection end it.
